The end-to-end combined carrier-to-noise density ratio is computed using inverse-linear addition:
\begin{equation}
    \left( \frac{C}{N_0} \right)_{\text{total,linear}} = \left( 10^{-84.08 / 10} + 10^{-84.25 / 10} \right)^{-1}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \left( \frac{C}{N_0} \right)_{\text{total,dBHz}} = 10\log_{10}\left( \left( \frac{C}{N_0} \right)_{\text{total,linear}} \right) = \cnTotal\ \text{dB-Hz}
\end{equation}
This gives $E_b/N_0=\ebnoTotal$~dB and a 10~Mbps fixed-rate margin of \textbf{\marginClear~dB} against the 7~dB requirement.

\subsubsection{Parametric Sensitivity and 2D Contour Map Analysis}
Five contour plots show the principal static sensitivities and link-closure boundaries.

\subsubsection[Downlink C/N0 vs. GS2 Dish Diameter and Frequency]{Downlink $(C/N_0)$ vs. GS2 Dish Diameter and Frequency}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{01_downlink_cn0_dish_vs_frequency.png}
    \caption{Downlink $(C/N_0)$ vs. GS2 dish diameter and frequency.}
    \label{fig:contour01}
\end{figure}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purpose:} Evaluate downlink $C/N_0$ over receive-dish diameters of 0.5--2.4~m and frequencies of 10.7--12.75~GHz.
    \item \textbf{Result:} $C/N_0$ ranges from approximately 81.5 to 91.5~dB-Hz over the plotted domain. The nearly vertical contours show that aperture is the dominant variable.
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} For fixed physical aperture and EIRP, receive gain and FSPL both contain an $f^2$ dependence, so their frequency terms largely cancel. Frequency becomes important when atmospheric loss, efficiency, or footprint EIRP also varies with band.
    \item \textbf{Design use:} The plot gives the minimum receive aperture needed for a specified $C/N_0$ under the fixed assumptions.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[Downlink Margin vs. Satellite EIRP and GS2 Tsys]{Downlink Margin vs. Satellite EIRP and GS2 $T_{\mathrm{sys}}$}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{02_downlink_margin_eirp_vs_tsys.png}
    \caption{Combined effective margin vs. satellite EIRP and GS2 $T_{\mathrm{sys}}$.}
    \label{fig:contour02}
\end{figure}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purpose:} Map the 0~dB end-to-end margin boundary over 42--58~dBW downlink EIRP and 90--330~K receiver noise temperature, with ASI and IMD enabled.
    \item \textbf{Numerical result:} The 0~dB contour separates closed and open combinations. At $T_{\mathrm{sys}}=300$~K, closure requires approximately 45.5~dBW. The selected 46~dBW, 150~K point gives approximately \marginASI~dB with ASI and IMD enabled.
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} Margin increases one-for-one with EIRP in dB, while the receiver term varies as $-10\log_{10}T_{\mathrm{sys}}$.
    \item \textbf{Design use:} The map quantifies the trade between leased downlink EIRP and receive-terminal $G/T$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Uplink C/N vs. HPA Power and GS1 Dish Diameter}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Design use:} The plot supports a cost and packaging trade between antenna size, amplifier rating, and off-axis discrimination.

\subsection{Advanced Impairment and Dynamic Analysis}
This section adds the optional interference, orbital, atmospheric, and adaptation models to the static reference case.
\subsubsection[Interference (ASI and IMD)]{Interference (ASI and IMD)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Purpose:} Evaluate ASI from modeled neighbors at $11^\circ$E and $15^\circ$E and add the assumed transponder IMD term.
    \item \textbf{Numerical result:} ASI and IMD reduce the margin from $\marginClear$~dB to \textbf{\marginASI~dB}. The ablation study attributes about 0.44~dB to ASI and 0.01~dB to the assumed IMD term.
    \item \textbf{Interpretation:} The combined 0.46~dB penalty is modest for the selected antenna sizes and assumed activity factor, but it is not a coordination result.
    \item \textbf{Design use:} An operational study would replace the generic sidelobe envelope and IMD assumption with measured antenna patterns, assigned polarizations, carrier powers, and transponder loading.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Performance Comparison and Engineering Synthesis}
The static and extended calculations use the same hardware and geometry inputs; only the enabled impairment and adaptation models change.

\begin{tcolorbox}[
    colback=gray!5,
    colframe=gray!45,
    title=Design Implication: Fixed-Rate Margin vs. ACM Throughput,
    fonttitle=\bfseries,
    boxrule=0.4pt,
    arc=2pt
]
\small
\textbf{Fixed-rate metric:} $E_b/N_0 = C/N_0 - 10\log_{10}(R_b)$ \\
\textbf{ACM metric:} $E_s/N_0 = C/N_0 - 10\log_{10}(R_s)$ \\
\textbf{Symbol rate:} $R_s = B / (1 + \alpha)$ \\
\textbf{Effective impaired metric:} Thermal noise and interference are combined using inverse-linear summation.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\textbf{Clarification:} The fixed-rate 10 Mbps case and the DVB-S2 ACM throughput case are not evaluated with the same closure criterion. The fixed-rate case uses a conservative required $E_b/N_0 = 7$ dB design threshold. The ACM case converts the available end-to-end $C/(N+I)_0$ into $E_s/N_0$ at the DVB-S2 symbol rate and checks closure against MODCOD-specific DVB-S2 thresholds plus implementation margin. Therefore, the ACM throughput values are estimated variable-rate DVB-S2 carrier capacities under the selected MODCOD, not a direct continuation of the fixed 10 Mbps operating mode.
\end{tcolorbox}

The ablation sequence shows that the advanced result is not driven by a single opaque ``advanced-model penalty.'' ASI accounts for approximately $0.44$~dB, whereas the selected IMD assumption contributes only about $0.01$~dB. At the 99.9\% design point, rain-induced carrier attenuation is the dominant incremental loss, and the associated increase in downlink sky-noise temperature contributes a further $0.91$~dB penalty. ACM does not restore the fixed-rate physical margin; it changes the operating threshold and throughput. The separate GEO-motion diagnostic produces only about $0.017$~dB peak-to-peak margin variation.

  \textbf{Interpretation of the Comparison:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Interference:} The selected ASI and IMD assumptions impose a combined penalty of approximately 0.46~dB. A coordination-grade value would require measured patterns and operator carrier data.
    \item \textbf{Atmospheric design point:} At 99.9\% target availability, the fixed-rate margin becomes \marginRain~dB. The baseline hardware therefore needs additional EIRP, receive aperture, power-control headroom, or a lower availability or rate requirement to close with the stated 7~dB threshold.
    \item \textbf{ACM response:} QPSK 1/2 remains selectable at the atmospheric design point and gives \throughputRain~Mbps for the 36~MHz carrier. This is a separate variable-rate operating mode and does not change the negative 10~Mbps fixed-rate margin.
\end{enumerate}

The clear-sky 10~Mbps link closes with positive margin. The selected interference assumptions cause a modest reduction, while the 99.9\% atmospheric case consumes the remaining fixed-rate margin. ACM provides a lower-threshold variable-rate mode, but the baseline configuration still does not demonstrate 99.9\% fixed-rate closure.
