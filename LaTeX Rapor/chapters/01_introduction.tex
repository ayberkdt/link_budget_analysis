\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Project Overview}
This term project presents a comprehensive analysis and simulation of a geostationary (GEO) satellite communication link. Modern satellite links require robust engineering calculations to size transmitter power, antenna diameters, and receiver noise performance. The objective of this study is two-fold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item To establish a mathematically transparent, MATLAB-comparable \textbf{Clear-Sky Static Link Budget} as the primary compulsory academic deliverable.
    \item To implement a series of advanced engineering extensions, including co-channel interference, digital baseband metrics, orbital motion, stochastic weather, standards-based \textbf{ITU-R atmospheric propagation sönümlemesi}, and \textbf{DVB-S2 Adaptive Coding and Modulation (ACM)} as professional extensions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scenario Definition}
The system models a one-way bent-pipe (transparent transponder) geostationary satellite link at Ku-band. The uydunun nominal yörünge konumu $42^\circ$ Doğu (Doğu boylamı) olarak atanmıştır. \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ground Station 1 (GS1 - Istanbul):} Broadcasting earth station acting as the uplink transmitter. Equipped with a $2.4$-meter parabolic dish, transmitting at $14.0$ GHz with a $20$ Watt high-power amplifier (HPA).
    \item \textbf{GEO Satellite (at 42E):} A transparent bent-pipe satellite that receives the uplink carrier at $14.0$ GHz and retransmits it on the downlink at $12.0$ GHz with a constant downlink EIRP footprint value of $50$ dBW.
    \item \textbf{Ground Station 2 (GS2 - Ankara):} A receive-only terminal acting as the downlink receiver. Equipped with a $0.9$-meter parabolic dish, operating with a receiver system noise temperature ($T_{sys}$) of $150$ K.
\end{itemize}

The transponder has a bandwidth of $36.0$ MHz, supporting a net information transmission rate of $10.0$ Mbps. The system uses a QPSK modulation with a $3/4$ inner LDPC code rate.
