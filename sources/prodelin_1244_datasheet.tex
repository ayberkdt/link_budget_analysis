\section*{General Description}
The Prodelin Series 1244 is a high-performance 2.4-meter VSAT antenna system featuring a 4-piece precision compression-molded SMC (Sheet Molding Compound) offset reflector. It is widely certified for global satellite networks including Intelsat and Eutelsat.
